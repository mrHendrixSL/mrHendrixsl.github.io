  <div>
    <h2 id="doctoral-heading">Modelling Intertextual Relations under Transformation</h2>
    <p>My doctoral research examines how textual relations can be represented, retrieved, judged, and explored when surface similarity is weakened through paraphrase, adaptation, obfuscation, allusion, historical variation, and other forms of textual change.</p>
    <p>The work combines information retrieval, language modelling, benchmark construction, evaluation, and visual analytics to support analysis across large textual collections.</p>
  </div>
</section>
