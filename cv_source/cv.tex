\section{Summary}
MSCA Doctoral Researcher at University College Cork, funded through the CASCADE Horizon Europe Marie Sk{\l}odowska-Curie Doctoral Network. My research focuses on the computational representation and retrieval of textual relations under transformation, particularly where lexical similarity is weakened or lost through paraphrase, adaptation, obfuscation, allusion, historical variation, and related forms of textual change.

My current work examines how such relations can be encoded, retrieved, ranked, and evaluated at scale across large textual collections. It includes semantic retrieval, parallel-text modelling, relation judgement, benchmark construction, and visual analytics for moving between corpus-level patterns and close reading.

Prior to my doctoral research, I worked for more than four years in data analytics and implementation engineering. From April to July 2026, I completed a research secondment at Nimbus Research Centre on grounding and trust in agentic cyber-physical systems within the inTrusted project.
