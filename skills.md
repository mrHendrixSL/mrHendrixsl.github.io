# Skills & Technologies

<div class="section-label">Current Research Stack</div>

<div class="card-grid">
  <div class="card">
    <div class="card-label">PhD Core</div>
    <h3>Parallel Text & Transformation</h3>
    <p>Corpus construction, relation schemas, candidate generation, text reuse detection, semantic change, and relation-space visualisation for translation, paraphrase, adaptation, simplification, and generated text.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <div class="card-label">Hiberno-English</div>
    <h3>Hybrid Extraction & Evaluation</h3>
    <p>Rule-based and LLM-assisted extraction from dictionary-style humanities data, with field normalisation, retry pipelines, proxy evaluation, agreement metrics, and manual audit workflows.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="card">
    <div class="card-label">Nimbus Secondment</div>
    <h3>Language-Mediated Trust</h3>
    <p>Trace analysis for agentic systems, including intent preservation across user instructions, handoffs, tool calls, protocol commands, and robot-side evidence in collaborative robotics settings.</p>
  </div>
</div>
